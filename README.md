# Awesome SolidCraft Plugins

A curated collection of plugins, extensions, and community tools for [SolidCraft CAD](https://github.com/tangency-labs/solidcraft).

Discover useful plugins, explore new features, and share your own projects.

## Plugins

### Fasteners & Threads

- [FDM Bolt & Nut Designer](https://github.com/EMAXX620/SolidCraft_FDM_Bolt_And_Nut_Designer) — Generate bolts and hex nuts for FDM printing, with configurable thread clearance and a choice of bolt head styles.

### Gears & Racks

- [SolidCraft Gears](https://github.com/bherbruck/solidcraft-gears) — Generate involute spur gears by specifying the module, tooth count, and face width. The gear is built with editable SolidCraft timeline features.
- [SolidCraft Gears & Racks](https://github.com/AliSintax/solidcraft-gears-racks) — A fork of SolidCraft Gears that adds straight racks and builds each gear or rack in its own editable component.
- [Gears by bbonsen](https://github.com/bbonsen/solidcraftGearsPlugin) — Create editable spur, helical, herringbone, bevel, and worm gears, plus planetary sets, racks, and cycloidal drives.

### Sketch Patterns

- [Honeycomb / Hexagon Generator](https://github.com/Xusr404/solidcraft-honeycomb-plugin) — Fill one or more sketch regions with a continuous hexagon pattern. Adjust the cell size and spacing, and optionally trim cells at the outline.

### Storage

- [Gridfinity Generator](https://github.com/Xusr404/solidcraft-gridfinity-generator) — Generate customizable Gridfinity storage bins and baseplates in SolidCraft.

### Woodworking

- [Woodwork](https://github.com/TomasDJo/solidcraft-woodwork) — Design parametric cabinets, add joinery, assign materials, and generate cut lists.

## Community Tools

### CNC & CAM

- [SolidCraft CAM for LinuxCNC](https://github.com/HansFel/SolidCraftCam) — A SolidCraft fork with built-in 2.5D CAM for generating toolpaths and exporting LinuxCNC-compatible G-code.
